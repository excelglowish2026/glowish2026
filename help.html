How to use Glowish Tracker

1. Logging in and out

  1. Open the website and type the username and password your admin gave you, then press Log in.
  2. The site sends you to the right dashboard automatically — you never choose it yourself. What you can see depends on your account type.
  3. When you are done, press Log out (top right). Always do this on a shared computer.

“Invalid username or password”? Check capital letters and spaces. If it still fails, ask your admin to check your row in the Credentials sheet.

2. Getting around — the basics

  • Tabs across the top (Price List, Ledger, …) switch between features. Click a tab to open it.
  • Pages of 30 — long lists show 30 rows at a time. Use Prev / Next under the table.
  • Refresh — press the Refresh button on a tab to reload the latest data from the spreadsheet.
  • Remarks — long remarks are shortened in the table. Click a remark to read the whole note in a pop-up.
  • Adding an entry — every tab has an Add form under its table. Fill in the fields, press the button, and the new row appears in the list.
  • Dates use the calendar picker. Always pick the real date of the transaction — weekly totals and reports depend on it.

3. Your store

Your account is tied to one store. The Store field in every Add form is already filled in and locked, and you only see your own store’s data. You cannot edit or delete rows after saving — if you made a mistake, tell your admin.

3. Your store access (“ALL”)

Your account covers every store in your region. Two things differ from a single-store account:

  • Tables show a Store column so you can tell stores apart.
  • The Store field in Add forms is not locked — type the store name exactly as it is spelled in the sheet (e.g. Cebu).
  • You also get two extra tabs: Weekly Collection and Rice Collection (see below).

You cannot edit or delete rows after saving — ask your admin.

Price List

Two views, switched with the toggle at the top: Regular Price and Promo Rates. Use the search box to find an item by name or category.

To add an item:

  1. Choose the toggle (Regular or Promo).
  2. Fill in Category, Item name and Price (or SRP for promos; promos also need a Promo name such as Christmas Sale).
  3. Pick the Discount from the dropdown.
  4. The Distributor price fills itself in — you cannot type in it. Press Add item.

Ledger

The ledger records each delivery, payment and balance per location. The newest balance for your store(s) appears in the list.

  1. Fill in Location, Date, Beginning balance, Reference, Delivery, Payment and Balance.
  2. Add a Remarks note if anything needs explaining (optional).
  3. Press Add entry.

Enter rows in the order they happened — the most recently added row is treated as the current balance.

Inventory

  • Pick a warehouse tab (e.g. Baybay, Quezon), then switch between Old items and New items.
  • A row with a Total under 10 is highlighted in red — time to restock.
  • To log stock movement: choose Category and the warehouse, enter the Item name, Beginning, In, Out and Total, plus who it went to, the address and who delivered it. Press Add item.
  • If a warehouse has nothing yet, the table says Empty Inventory.

Collectibles

Collectibles are organised by Province then City.

  1. Click a province tab (e.g. Cebu), then a city tab under it (e.g. Mandaue).
  2. The list shows that city’s entries grouped by collection week, Saturday to Friday (for example Aug 29 – Sep 4), each week with its own total, and an overall total at the bottom.
  3. To add an entry, make sure the right province and city tabs are selected, then fill in Date, Name, Address and Balance and press Add collection.

Generate Report (under the list):

  • By city — every week for the selected city, with totals.
  • By province — choose a month to get one total per city for that province.
  • Press Generate report, check the preview, then Export report (CSV) (opens in Excel) or (PDF).

If you see “No provinces yet”, an admin still has to add cities. Staff cannot add new provinces or cities.

Weekly Collection Store = ALL only

Tracks money collected from distributors and the expenses paid out, one Monday-to-Saturday week at a time.

  1. Pick any date inside the week you want and press Load week. The page shows that week’s range (e.g. May 11 – May 16).
  2. The three cards at the top — Total collections, Total expenses, Net — update instantly.
  3. Use the Collections / Expenses toggle to switch lists. Use the search box to find a name quickly.
  4. Add collection: Store, Date, Distributor name, Amount, Contact person/number, Remarks. Add expense: Store, Date, Particular, Amount.

Generate Report: choose By week or By month, pick any date or month (it does not have to be the week you are viewing), press Generate report, then export as CSV or PDF.

Rice Collection Store = ALL only

Rice collections are listed per municipality, in Saturday-to-Friday weeks that restart on the first Saturday of every month.

  • Weekly View — click a municipality tab, load a week, and add entries (Date, Name, Address, Balance). A District Summary under the list totals each municipality for that week.
  • View List (by month) — the full history of the selected municipality, grouped month by month, oldest first.
  • District Report — pick a district and a month to get a table of municipalities down the side and weeks across the top, with totals and an overall total. Export it as CSV or PDF.

If a municipality is missing from the tabs, ask an admin to add it.

Exporting to Excel or PDF

  • CSV / Excel: the file downloads straight away. Open it with Excel (or Google Sheets).
  • PDF: a new tab opens with a clean report and your browser’s Print window appears. Change the destination to Save as PDF, then press Save. The site does not create a PDF file by itself.
  • If nothing happens, your browser may be blocking the pop-up — allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

Admin dashboard — Overview

As an admin you see every region and store. The Overview tab shows:

  • Number cards — active stores, price list items, outstanding ledger balance, outstanding collectibles, inventory items, low-stock items, plus the all-time weekly collection total, expenses and net income.
  • Low stock card — click it to jump to Inventory showing only items under 10 units. Press Clear filter to go back.
  • By region — a small summary for Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.

Press Refresh all data (top right) after you or someone else has made changes.

Price List, Ledger, Inventory

  • These tabs show data from all three regions merged together, with a Region column. Use the search box to filter; the counter shows how many rows match.
  • Every row has Edit and Delete links. Edit loads the row into the form below, then press Update entry (or Cancel edit). Delete asks you to confirm and cannot be undone.
  • In the Add form, choose the Region first — the row is saved to that region’s spreadsheet.
  • Price List has Regular Price / Promo Rates toggles; distributor price is calculated automatically from the discount.
  • Inventory has warehouse tabs and an Old/New toggle. Press + Add warehouse to create a new warehouse tab for everyone.

Collectibles (admin)

  • Same Province → City tabs and Saturday–Friday week grouping that staff see, but across all regions, with search and Edit/Delete on each row.
  • Press + Add city to add a new city and its province. A brand-new province gets its own tab automatically.
  • Generate Report — By city or By province (pick a month), exported as CSV or PDF.

Reports tab

Pick a Week ending date and press Generate to see Weekly Sales (from the Ledger: entries, delivery, payment, ending balance per store) and Weekly Stock Inventory. Each block has its own Export CSV button.

Weekly Collection and Rice Collection (admin)

  • Weekly Collection works like the staff version but covers every region, with a Region column and Edit/Delete. Use Generate Report (by week or month) for CSV/PDF exports.
  • Rice Collection has the same Weekly View, View List and District Report modes. Press + Add municipality to add a municipality (it asks for the name, province and district).
  • Both tabs have a search box above the list.

Insurance (owner only)

The Insurance tab is private — no other account can see it.

  • Choose a month tab (January–December) to see that month’s records, newest first.
  • Add records with Month, Date, Name, Birthdate, Address and Remarks. Choosing a date picks the matching month for you.
  • Edit or delete a record from its row.
  • Press Export to Excel (CSV) to download the records.

Managing accounts

Logins live in the Credentials sheet of the Directory spreadsheet (Username, Password, Role, Region, Store). To create a staff login, add a row with Role staff, the Region, and either one Store name or ALL. Admins use Role admin; the business owner uses owner. The site reads the sheet at login, so new accounts work immediately.

Troubleshooting

I added something but it does not show.
Press Refresh. If a recent site update still looks old, do a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
A week or month looks empty but I know there is data.
Check the date range shown beside the date picker, and that the entry’s date is filled in.
“Couldn’t load” or “Not connected to a spreadsheet”.
Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, tell your admin.
I can’t see a tab my coworker has.
Tabs depend on your account type (for example Weekly and Rice Collection need Store = ALL). Ask your admin if your access should change.
I need to fix a mistake.
Staff cannot edit saved rows — ask an admin, who can edit or delete it.
